RAQEEB رقيب

AI-Assisted Smart-Meter Analysis & Case Management

RAQEEB is designed to help electricity providers turn large amounts of smart-meter consumption data into clear, prioritized cases for human review.

01 / The problem

Consumption anomalies can be difficult to identify and review manually when large volumes of readings, alerts and follow-up records are disconnected.

02 / The approach

Classify consumption as normal or potentially suspicious, explain why patterns were flagged, assign risk-based priority and support organized case follow-up.

03 / The value

Help analysts and supervisors focus on important cases, understand the model output and record investigation outcomes without treating an automated flag as proof of theft.
